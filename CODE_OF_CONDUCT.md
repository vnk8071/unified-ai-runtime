# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful place to work on inference runtimes, whatever your experience, background or employer.

Expected: be considerate, assume good faith, give specific and kind feedback, accept review of your own work, and say plainly what you
did and did not verify.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's private information, and
sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a
problem, contact the maintainer through the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or the email on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
